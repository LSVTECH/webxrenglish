Why LinguaSpace works

Traditional English practice lacks the pressure and realism of real interactions. We change that.

🧠
AI-Powered Guests

Each scenario features an AI guest with a unique personality — polite, impatient, indecisive, or demanding. You never practice the same conversation twice.

🎯
Mission-Based Learning

Each experience has 6 specific missions to complete. Clear objectives keep you focused and let you measure real progress with every session.

📊
Detailed Evaluation

After each session, receive scores across 5 criteria: Communication, Logical Order, Professionalism, Precision, and Fluency — plus actionable recommendations.

How it works

Three steps from login to improvement.

01
Choose Your Scenario

Log in and select from available VR experiences. Each scenario targets specific vocabulary and conversation structures used in real professional situations.

02
Speak, Listen, React

Use your voice to interact with an AI guest inside a VR environment. Complete all 6 missions in the correct order — just like a real interaction would unfold.

03
Review & Improve

Receive a detailed evaluation with scores across 5 criteria plus specific recommendations. Your professor can track your progress across all sessions over time.
